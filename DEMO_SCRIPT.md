# Pocket Site Demo Script

Target length: about 2 minutes 30 seconds.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

“A garage-sale sign tells you where a sale is, but not what is actually there. Marketplace sites solve discovery; Pocket Site helps people browse a temporary physical place.”

Show the Pocket Site Draft screen.

## 0:20–0:45 — Prepare the sale

1. Tap **Load Demo Sale** and confirm.
2. Briefly show the 10 believable items, categories, prices, and mixed availability.
3. Point out sale notes, optional contact handoff, and the Looking For list.

## 0:45–1:15 — Open and share

1. Tap **Open Sale**.
2. Allow notifications if prompted.
3. Show the prominent Open state and persistent “Pocket Site sale is live” notification.
4. Show the PocketWiFiSale join QR, catalog QR/link, and copyable physical-sign instructions.
5. Explain: visitors join the designated sale Wi-Fi, then open the local catalog. No app or internet account is needed.

## 1:15–1:50 — Visitor experience

1. On a second phone already connected to PocketWiFiSale, scan/open the catalog.
2. Show the **Read-only visitor catalog** label.
3. Browse items and point out that available items appear before visually distinct sold items.
4. Show notes, contact handoff, categories, and Looking For content.
5. On the host phone, mark one available item sold.
6. Let the visitor page auto-refresh within eight seconds and show the updated sold state.

## 1:50–2:15 — Background stability and closure

1. Briefly switch the host to another app and point out the ongoing notification.
2. Confirm the visitor catalog remains available.
3. Return to Pocket Site and tap **Close Sale**.
4. Refresh the visitor phone and explain that local serving and its notification stop when the sale closes.

## 2:15–2:30 — Temporary by design

1. Tap **Delete All Sale Data** and confirm.
2. Show the empty Draft state.
3. Close with: “Pocket Site is local-first, private, and temporary by design—not another marketplace.”

## Recording setup

- Configure the hotspot or guest network before recording.
- Connect both phones and verify device-to-device traffic.
- Grant Pocket Site notification permission.
- Test the QR/link once before the final take.
- Pocket Site shows categories but does not include visitor filtering in this v1 build; say “browse,” not “filter.”
